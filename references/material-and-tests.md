# Silicone-Gel Claim and Test Mapping

Use this reference to select visuals. Choose only tests that directly support the user's words; a short video does not need the whole catalog.

## Material Scope

This skill concerns cured silicone gel used to pot or encapsulate LED assemblies in explosion-proof and outdoor luminaires. Silicone gels vary widely. Never infer cure chemistry, mix ratio, cure time, hardness, adhesion, flame rating, operating range, thermal conductivity, optical transmission, or electrical properties without product data.

## Claim-to-Visual Map

| Copy meaning | Preferred visual | Physical result to show | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 透明、透光 | Macro glide across clear cured gel with LEDs or a black-white grid visible beneath | Clear, colorless, bubble-free layer; LEDs remain optically legible | Fake numeric transmittance |
| 不易黄变、耐UV | UV fluorescent or xenon ageing chamber, followed by before/after specimens under controlled lighting and a colorimeter | Aged specimen remains visually clear relative to a control | Claiming zero yellowing forever; invented exposure hours or ΔE/YI |
| 柔软不脆裂 | Flat compression probe or broad fingertip/palm pressing a cured slab | Broad shallow indentation, coherent deformation, rapid recovery, no cracks or crumbs | Deep liquid-like wobble, pointy peaks, stringing |
| 回弹、应力缓冲 | Compression/recovery or repeated low-amplitude loading; optional cross-section around components | Gel deforms around parts and returns without debonding | Unverified quantitative damping claims |
| 拉伸性好、高延伸 | Universal tensile machine pulling a cured dumbbell or wide strip | Uniform elongation without immediate fracture, followed by controlled recovery | Pulling gel out of the actual lamp; hair-thin filaments; invented percentages |
| 不拉丝 | Hand or probe separates cleanly from the installed cured surface | Surface stays below or at its original plane and snaps back immediately | Any upward-following peak, column, thread, or slime behavior |
| 防水 | Fully assembled luminaire in a controlled IP-style water-spray or water-jet rig | Water strikes from relevant directions; lamp remains stable; post-shot interior stays dry | Using immersion unless claimed; assigning an IP grade without evidence |
| 防潮、耐湿热 | Controlled damp-heat chamber; double-85 display only when requested | No visible moisture ingress, corrosion, clouding, delamination, or electrical instability | Treating humidity as a water-jet test; inventing test duration |
| 耐高温、耐低温 | Hot or cold chamber, optionally followed by inspection | Gel remains intact and clear; no melt, embrittlement, bubbling, or delamination | Open flame as a temperature test; invented temperature limits |
| 冷热循环不易裂 | Temperature-cycling chamber with controlled hot/cold transitions and macro inspection | No cracking, crazing, edge lifting, shrinkage, or loss of LED function | A single heat-gun pass presented as thermal cycling |
| 耐振动、减震 | Instrumented shaker table with mounted luminaire or potted board | Assembly remains secure and LEDs stable; gel cushions components | Violent cinematic shaking or unverified test severity |
| 耐冲击 | Controlled mechanical shock/impact fixture if the copy explicitly claims it | No visible separation or component damage | Confusing IK enclosure impact with gel performance |
| 防尘 | Dust chamber around an assembled, closed luminaire | No visible internal dust after exposure | Labeling IP6X without product evidence |
| 绝缘、介电性好 | Safe laboratory dielectric/insulation-resistance setup using electrodes and meters | Stable instrument reading, no dramatic arc | Invented kV values; unsafe exposed high-voltage spectacle |
| 导热 | Controlled comparative thermal image only when the formulation actually claims thermal conductivity | More uniform heat distribution or lower hotspot relative to a valid control | Assuming every silicone gel is thermally conductive |
| 阻燃 | Standardized flammability setup only with documented rating | Restrained laboratory shot and accurate supplied rating | Claiming UL 94 level or using dramatic burning without evidence |
| 耐盐雾、耐腐蚀 | Salt-spray chamber for outdoor/marine claims, normally focused on the assembled system | No visible corrosion or seal failure after documented exposure | Attributing enclosure corrosion resistance solely to gel |
| 易返修、可剥离 | Controlled removal from a test coupon or serviceable module only when supported | Cohesive, clean removal without board damage | Assuming all gels are re-enterable |

## Standards-Inspired Visuals

These references guide credible art direction; they are not permission to claim compliance:

- Enclosure water/dust protection: IEC 60529; luminaire ingress tests also relate to IEC 60598-1.
- Damp heat: IEC 60068-2-78 for steady-state damp heat; accelerated damp heat may use other parts such as IEC 60068-2-67 depending on specimen and plan.
- Temperature change/cycling: IEC 60068-2-14.
- Vibration and shock: IEC 60068-2-6 and IEC 60068-2-27.
- UV weathering of polymeric materials: ISO 4892-3 or ASTM G154; xenon-arc approaches may use ISO 4892-2.
- Tensile properties of vulcanized or thermoplastic rubber-like specimens: ISO 37 or ASTM D412, subject to material applicability.
- Explosion-protected equipment: IEC 60079 series. Never imply the potting gel alone establishes an Ex rating.

If exact editions, conditions, pass criteria, or certification language matter, verify the current authoritative publication before writing them.

## Visual Continuity

- Match the reference product in every scene. Keep housing geometry, LEDs, screws, gasket, cable gland, colors, and proportions stable.
- A detached optical cover is not a second potting tray. Do not fill it unless explicitly requested.
- Testing an assembled luminaire and testing a cured material coupon are different visuals. Use the assembled product for ingress/system tests; use standardized specimens for tensile, compression, UV comparison, and intrinsic material tests.
- Use time-lapse or a clean cut to show curing. Do not portray unknown cure chemistry as instantaneous reaction, heat flash, smoke, or crystallization.
